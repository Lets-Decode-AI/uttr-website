Mode: Persuade. Visitor: the four PRODUCT.md audiences. Action: download the right installer for this visitor's system (live from GitHub). Proof: the dictation itself, drawn; measured 421 ms key-up latency; open source and release notes. No generated imagery; graphics are drawn in code. No competitor names.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Speech you can see. The voice is drawn as it is heard, floats across the visitor's own desk and settles as text at the cursor of whatever app they use. Refuses the category default: centred headline over an app screenshot and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Spectrograph grid paper (#e2e9e6, faint forest 32 px grid), forest ink (#112a21, #274b40), mint highlight (#a8e0c1) only for freshly inserted text, white app sheets (#fcfdfc). A second ground, forest ink (#112a21), is reserved for the two bands that carry trust and action: the privacy map and the download table (and the footer that closes the download band). Flat overlapping sheets, no shadows or gradients. Urbanist for all text; Shantell Sans italic for exactly one stressed word per heading and for hand-written leader-line notes; Martian Mono for axis ticks, versions and file names. Waveform bars and spectrogram bands drawn in SVG/canvas.

STORY: First, what it does (hold, speak, let go, text lands anywhere). Then how it does it fast (chunks cut at pauses, drawn as a spectrogram band). Then why it is safe (what runs where). Then what else it does, the models, the release notes, and the download table.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: pill tags, the h1 "Say it once. It lands wherever you type." with "wherever" in Shantell, the lede, the detected-OS download button and version meta. Right seven columns: a drawn laptop whose desk holds Slack, Mail and Notes sheets; the Flow Bar at the bottom of the desk; a waveform ribbon rises from it and becomes text at the active caret, cycling apps. Holding the on-page key records into the ribbon.
